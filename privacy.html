Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 May 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Luxury Mindset Scheduler ("App") handles information. The App is a single-creator automation tool that publishes content to the @wealthbyluxury TikTok account. It is not a public-facing service and does not collect personal data from end users.

1. No Personal Data Collection

The App does not collect, store, or process personal data from any third-party users. The App is operated exclusively by its creator for their own content scheduling purposes.

2. TikTok API Data

The App uses the TikTok Content Posting API to publish videos to the creator's own TikTok account. The following data is accessed:

  • TikTok access token – stored locally on the creator's device to authenticate API requests. It is never shared with third parties.
  • TikTok Open ID – used solely to identify the creator's own account for posting.

No TikTok user data belonging to third parties (followers, viewers, commenters) is collected or stored.

3. Third-Party Services

The App integrates with the following third-party services for content creation and publishing:

  • Anthropic (Claude AI) – generates content scripts. See Anthropic Privacy Policy.
  • ElevenLabs – generates voiceover audio. See ElevenLabs Privacy Policy.
  • Pexels – provides royalty-free stock footage. See Pexels Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare R2 – temporarily hosts video files for TikTok to pull. See Cloudflare Privacy Policy.
  • TikTok – receives published video content via the Content Posting API. See TikTok Privacy Policy.

4. Data Storage

API credentials and access tokens are stored locally in a .env file on the creator's personal device. No data is stored on external databases or shared servers beyond what is necessary for the above third-party integrations.

5. Cookies

This website (GitHub Pages) does not use cookies or tracking technologies.

6. Children's Privacy

This App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes.

8. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, please contact us via Instagram: @wealthbyluxury
